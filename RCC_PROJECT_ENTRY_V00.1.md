---

## 1. Purpose

This document is the first functional entry point to the public RCC project structure.

Its purpose is not to provide a complete description of RCC, nor to establish a canonical architecture.

It tests a simple public information path:

External Communication -> GitHub Entry Point -> Structured Technical Knowledge

The structure introduced here is expected to evolve as the project, its terminology, experimental evidence, and public documentation develop.

---

## 2. Public Knowledge Structure

The initial public organization is divided into provisional layers:

### L0 — ENTRY
Orientation and navigation into the RCC public knowledge base.

### L1 — FOUNDATIONS
Foundational concepts and the conceptual basis from which RCC develops.

### L2 — CONCEPTS & TERMINOLOGY
Definitions, semantic evolution, terminology, and concept genealogy.

### L3 — ARCHITECTURE
Modules, boundaries, operational structures, and architectural relationships.

### L4 — REALIZATIONS
Concrete software, representational objects, implementations, and physical realizations.

### L5 — EXPERIMENTS
Controlled experiments used to test architectural and representational hypotheses.

### L6 — EVIDENCE & PROVENANCE
Experimental evidence, provenance, reproducibility records, chronology, and traceability.

These layers are provisional and non-canonical.

---

## 3. Epistemic Principle

RCC public documentation distinguishes between:

- hypothesis,
- experimental realization,
- observed evidence,
- frozen experimental result,
- and promoted or canonical knowledge.

The existence of a concept or implementation does not imply that it has been established as a foundational principle.

In particular:

**FROZEN does not imply CANONICAL.**

---

## 4. Development Principle

The public structure follows the same general discipline used in the technical project:

Concept -> Representation -> Realization -> Experiment -> Evidence -> Revision

Implementation and experimental evidence are allowed to modify or contradict earlier conceptual formulations.

For this reason, historical versions and conceptual genealogy are relevant parts of the project rather than documentation noise.

---

## 5. Current Scope

V00.1 establishes only the public entry mechanism and the first layered information structure.

It does not attempt to:

- fully define RCC,
- freeze the final repository architecture,
- establish a complete terminology,
- claim completeness,
- or canonize the current organization.

Its immediate mission is narrower:

**Demonstrate that RCC knowledge can be exposed publicly through a structured, traceable, and progressively extensible technical path.**

---

## 6. Evolution

Future versions may introduce or reorganize:

- foundational documents,
- terminology records,
- concept genealogy,
- architectural descriptions,
- experimental series,
- evidence packages,
- provenance records,
- and public technical articles.

Changes to this entry point should remain versioned and traceable.

---

## 7. Experimental Status

This entry point should itself be treated as a realization under evaluation.

The working sequence is:

PROPOSE -> REALIZE -> USE -> OBSERVE -> REVISE

Therefore:

**RCC_PROJECT_ENTRY_V00.1 is a functional public-entry experiment, not a final declaration of the RCC knowledge architecture.**
