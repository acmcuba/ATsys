# RCC — Representation-Centric Computing

## ATsys ARE Research Project

RCC is an experimental research initiative exploring
representation-centric approaches to computing systems.

The project investigates how structured representations,
relational structures, mission-oriented objectives,
invariants, and temporal observations may contribute
to the organization and evaluation of computational systems.

## Research Areas

- Representation-centric computing
- Relational structures
- Mission-oriented representations
- Vector-based conceptual models
- Representational genealogy
- Invariant-based evaluation
- Temporal observation
- Experimental reproducibility

## Research Status

RCC is an evolving experimental research project.

Conceptual proposals, experimental observations,
and validated results are distinguished explicitly.

The publication of a conceptual model does not imply
that its implementation or performance has been demonstrated.
