# RCC — Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

Representation-Centric Computing (RCC) is a research
initiative investigating computational organization
through structured representations and their relationships.

The research considers representations not merely
as passive data objects, but as elements that may
participate in computational organization.

## 2. Research Motivation

Traditional computational systems commonly separate
data representation from the procedures operating
on that data.

RCC investigates alternative models in which
representational structure, relational context,
mission objectives, and invariants can be studied
within a common conceptual framework.

## 3. Research Dimensions

### Structure

The organization of representational elements.

### Function

The operations associated with those elements.

### Relations

The connections and dependencies among
representational elements.

### Mission

The objectives and constraints used to evaluate
a particular representational configuration.

### Time

The temporal context associated with observation,
execution, and persistence.

## 4. Research Approach

The project follows an incremental experimental approach.

Each experiment should identify:

- Its objective.
- Its initial conditions.
- The modification introduced.
- The evidence collected.
- The validation criteria.
- The limitations of the results.

## 5. Scope

This document introduces the conceptual research
framework only.

It does not describe proprietary implementations
or establish experimental validation of the
complete RCC architecture.
