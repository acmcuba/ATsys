# RCC — Conceptual Lexicon V00.1

## Representation

A structured description of an entity, state,
condition, or relationship within a defined
computational context.

## Vector

A structured mathematical object used to represent
multiple components or dimensions associated
with a state, observation, or transformation.

## Genealogy

The documented relationship between a representational
element and its antecedents, transformations,
or derived elements.

## Invariant

A condition expected to remain satisfied
within a specified operational or experimental scope.

## Mission

A defined set of objectives, constraints,
and evaluation conditions associated with
a representational process.

## Relational Structure

An organization of representational elements
connected through explicitly defined relationships.

## Temporal Observation

The recording of temporal information associated
with states, events, or representational operations.

## Representational Sufficiency

The condition under which a representation
contains enough information to satisfy
a specified evaluation requirement.

## Experimental Baseline

A documented reference configuration used
to evaluate changes and preserve reproducibility.

## Evidence

Recorded information supporting a specific
experimental observation or claim.

---

These definitions constitute an initial research
vocabulary and may be refined through documented
versions.

They do not imply exclusive ownership of
general mathematical or scientific terminology.
