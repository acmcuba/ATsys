# RCC — Research Methodology

## Experimental Discipline

RCC research follows an incremental methodology
based on controlled modifications and
observable evidence.

## Core Principles

### 1. Minimal Experimental Change

Each experimental stage should introduce
a clearly identified modification.

### 2. Baseline Preservation

Stable experimental configurations should
remain identifiable and reproducible.

### 3. Explicit Evidence

Experimental conclusions should reference
observable records.

### 4. Separation of Observation and Interpretation

Recorded observations must remain distinguishable
from hypotheses and interpretations.

### 5. Reproducibility

Experimental documentation should identify
the conditions necessary to repeat a test.

### 6. Temporal Traceability

Where relevant, observations should preserve
their temporal context.

### 7. Scope of Validation

A successful test validates only the properties
actually examined.

## Publication Requirements

Experimental publications should identify:

- Experiment identifier.
- Research objective.
- Experimental conditions.
- Observations.
- Validation results.
- Limitations.
- Evidence references, when approved for release.

## Research Integrity

A conceptual proposal must not be presented
as an experimentally demonstrated result.

A successful documentary integrity check
must not be presented as functional certification.
